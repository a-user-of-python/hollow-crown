The Hollow Crown

A 2D campaign running the real Dungeons & Dragons 2024 rules (SRD 5.2.1).

Forty years ago, King Corvin Alder fell in the War of the Ashen Field, and his crown — the Hollow Crown, sworn to bind the loyalty of Aldermere's war-dead — was shattered and lost. Now a pale shepherdess walks the fields at dusk, counting the graves. She means to gather the Crown's three shards and raise the Ashen Host, so that no living child ever marches to war again.

She has not counted on you.

Six chapters · Levels 1–8 · Real dice, real rules: Weapon Mastery, death saves, concentration, the lot.
Rules content from the SRD 5.2.1, CC-BY 4.0, Wizards of the Coast. Story, places and people are original. No blood or gore.
